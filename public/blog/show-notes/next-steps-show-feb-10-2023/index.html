Episode Summary

This week our conversation included Attorney Kristine Demo-Vazquez, candidate for Monroe County Family Court Judge.

Kristine is someone who we’ve come to admire. Someone who raised a very large family, including caring for her mom(s) (emphasis on the plurality of the word), and built a very successful law firm from scratch. She has been and remains counsel to thousands of families and children, and is a candidate for Monroe County Family Court Judge.

Check out her websites at: DemoVazquezLaw.com and KristineforFamilyCourt.com

Listen in to learn what Next Steps one person has taken for the betterment of others.
