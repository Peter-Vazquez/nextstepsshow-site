Episode Summary

He was driving home with his three-year-old son in the back seat when the bullets hit.

Terrorists opened fire with AK-47s on a quiet Israeli road, leaving David Rubin bleeding, his car crippled, and his son—shot in the head—silent in shock. But that night didn’t end in death. It became the beginning of something far more powerful: a voice that refuses to stay silent.

On this episode of The Next Steps Show, Peter Vazquez sits down with the Honorable David Rubin, former mayor of Shiloh, Israel, terror survivor, and founder of the Shiloh Israel Children’s Fund. And they don’t waste a second.

Broadcasting straight from Israel, Rubin tells the story the media won’t touch. From personal tragedy to national survival, he pulls back the curtain on the real enemies of Judeo-Christian civilization—radical Islam, the far-left alliance, and the global institutions funding both.

Rubin lays it bare: Iran isn’t just Israel’s enemy. It’s the puppet master funding terror across the Middle East and beyond. Hamas isn’t a resistance—it's a death cult. And the so-called “progressive” American left? They’re playing right into their hands, blindly chanting slogans they don’t understand while siding with regimes that would jail or kill them for their beliefs.

This episode isn’t about headlines. It’s about history. It’s about faith, clarity, and courage in an upside-down world.

• Why Israel will never allow Iran to go nuclear

• The real reason Marxists and jihadists are aligned

• What American college students don't know—and how it’s killing us

• Why President Trump was right to stand firm

• And how healing broken children became David Rubin’s life’s work

This is more than a political discussion. It’s a spiritual and cultural wake-up call for anyone who still believes in truth, family, and the God-given right to defend both.

Because liberty is not negotiated, it is defended.
