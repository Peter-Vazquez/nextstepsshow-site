Episode Summary

We are thrilled to introduce a truly inspiring individual who, just like you and me, began her journey as an everyday "Joe"—or rather, "Josephina." Through her determination and passion, she has found her balance and is now a Certified Integrative Nutrition Health Coach. She is the heart and soul behind Healthy Rican, known lovingly as the Nutrition Dork.

Our guest, Mayra Luz Colón, infuses her Puerto Rican heritage into healthy eating, turning everyday meals into vibrant culinary experiences. With her published cookbook and unwavering mission to banish bland foods, she empowers women to embrace their wellness journeys with flavor and joy.
