Episode Summary

Dreams Without Reason – Delusion, Dependency, and the Death of Values

No guest. No filter. Just Peter Vazquez at the mic - taking the gloves off.

Peter confronts a culture losing its grip on truth. From Harvard’s double standard academic freedom to New York City’s delusional billion-dollar bathroom plan, Peter exposes what happens when dreams are no longer anchored in reason and when reason is stripped of moral clarity.

He challenges the modern mindset that trades hard-earned wisdom for victimhood, questions the abuse of SNAP benefits, and calls out the quiet tyranny of policies built on emotion, not Constitution. This isn’t about left or right, it’s about right and wrong.

It’s raw. It’s bold. And it’s 100% Peter—calling America back to faith, reason, and responsibility.
