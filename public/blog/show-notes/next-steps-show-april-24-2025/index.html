Episode Summary

They call it momentum. We call it Van-bōōl-zal-ness—the crisis of deception dressed as progress. While Malik Evans touts’ feel-good slogans, the reality on Rochester’s streets tells a different story.

From race-baiting speeches to equity theater, this isn’t leadership—it’s gaslighting at a government scale. We’re not here to clap—we’re here to call it out.

Peter takes the gloves off and exposes the hollow hope and orchestrated crisis plaguing our cities. Featuring Malcolm X truth bombs, real talk on volunteerism, and a takedown of the myth of momentum. If you’re tired of the lies, the labels, andthe legacy of liberal failure—tune in. This is where we fight back with faith, facts, and fire.
