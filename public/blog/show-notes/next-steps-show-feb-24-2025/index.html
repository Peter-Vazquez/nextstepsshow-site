Episode Summary

THEY WANT YOU DISTRACTED. DON’T FALL FOR IT.

The left plays the same tired game—dangle race, January 6th, and political prisoners in front of us while they burn the country down behind our backs. They have spent four years weaponizing the justice system, and now that the truth is clawing its way to the surface, they are screaming that they are the victims? Give me a break.

We have seen the footage. We have heard the whistleblowers. The so-called "insurrectionists" were waved in, not waging war. But facts do not matter to those who thrive on fear and control. They silence the truth, punish the dissenters, and propped up a Justice Department that had become a political hit squad—until Pam Bondi stepped in to clean house.

And now? Governors are sending in the military to intimidate employees who refuse to bow. The left is clutching its pearls over Trump’s promise of justice because they know what is coming. The real America—our America—is waking up.

We are done playing defense. Done apologizing. Done letting them sell victimhood as a way of life. We stand for God, family, country. We stand for perseverance, for truth, for accountability. And when they come after our people, our values, our freedom—we stand up and fight back.

Kash Patel is calling for accountability. Dan Bongino is standing firm. And we? We are just getting started.
