Episode Summary

They’re not coming for your guns. They’re coming for your rights—and the guns are just in the way.

On this powerful episode of The Next Steps Show, GOA National Director Mark Jones joins the fight against America’s ideological decay. From Hawaii’s shady gun bans to New York’s Safe Act circus, we expose the assault on the Second Amendment, the racial manipulation tactics fueling it, and the silence of voters who should know better.
