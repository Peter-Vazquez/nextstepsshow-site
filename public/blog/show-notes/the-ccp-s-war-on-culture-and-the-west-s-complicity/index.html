Episode Summary

What happens when a regime erases faith, family, and freedom—while the West watches in silence?

On this gripping episode of The Next Steps Show, Shen Yun conductor Ying Chen joins the fight for truth, shedding light on China’s brutal persecution of Falun Gong, state-sponsored cultural erasure, and the creeping silence of American complicity.

From labor camps to propaganda, from spiritual genocide to tech surveillance, this isn’t just China’s problem. It's a preview of what happens when governments gain unchecked power over life, culture, and conscience.
