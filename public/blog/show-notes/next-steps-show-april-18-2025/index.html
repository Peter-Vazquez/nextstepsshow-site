Episode Summary

Easter isn’t just about an empty tomb—it’s about full lives.

On this explosive episode of The Next Steps Show, we confront what it really means to live like the resurrection is real.

Pastor Mike Hennessy joins Peter Vazquez to throw down truth in the public square—faith in the marketplace, fatherhood under fire, biblical manhood under siege, and the resurrection as a cultural reckoning, not just a Sunday morning slogan.

If Christ rose, then everything changes—government doesn’t get the last word, culture doesn’t define truth, and weak leadership has no place at the table.

From the battlefield to the boardroom, and from personal loss to public witness, this show is about standing firm. No fear. No compromise. No apologies. He is risen. Let’s live like it.
