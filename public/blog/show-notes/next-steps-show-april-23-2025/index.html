Episode Summary

They said the people’s house was too dangerous. That innocence was outdated. That tradition had to give way to “progress.” But we strip away the noise and expose what’s really going on, a quiet unraveling of everything that once held us together.

From the White House lawn to the classroom desk, the message is clear: what used to be sacred is now mocked, and what once required moral courage is now labeled as hate.

But not on our watch. With voices from the ground, parents, patriots, and educators, we confront the crisis not just of policy, but of purpose. It’s not just confusion in our classrooms or fences around our symbols, it’s the systematic flipping of right and wrong, good and evil, order and chaos.

If you’ve felt like the world’s upside-down, you’re not alone. But you are needed. Tune in and take the next step - toward truth, tradition, and turning things right-side up.
