Episode Summary

Join Peter Vazquez and Ayesha Kreutz on the Next Steps Show for an educational, and fun conversation about Next Steps to overcoming issues eroding economics, politicos, and freedoms of all Americans.

This week our conversation included the Hon. Rev. James R. Harden, M.Div., the CEO of CompassCare Pregnancy Services, and lives outside of Rochester, NY with his wife and ten children.

Rev. Harden pioneered the first measurable and repeatable medical model in the pregnancy center movement, helping over 650 centers nationwide become more effective at reaching more women and saving more babies from abortion. He has written extensively on medical ethics, executive leadership, and pro-life strategy.

CompassCare’s pro-life medical office in Buffalo was firebombed on June 7th, reopening a miraculous 52 days after the brutal attack. Since then, he has tirelessly spoken in the public square for women and pre-born babies. He has been a featured guest on Hannity, Huckabee, Glenn Beck, Eric Metaxas, Ministry Now with Joni Lamb, Focus Today with Perry Atkinson, Washington Watch with Tony Perkins and scores of other national radio and TV shows.

We’re a show on a mission with a vision for a community enhanced by dialog, teaching the balance Faith, Politics, and Entrepreneurship. A society where we grow by forgiving yesterday’s ills and teach overcoming barriers vs. succumbing to victimhood – an emotional intelligent but empathetic culture. A culture that values life. A culture where government empowers not enables. CompassCare.info
