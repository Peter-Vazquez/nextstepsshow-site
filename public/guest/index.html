Guest Inquiries

Guests should be prepared for meaningful conversation on issues that affect families, communities, businesses, culture, faith, and civic life.

Ideal guests bring a clear message, real experience, and a willingness to speak plainly. The show values thoughtful disagreement, principled discussion, and practical solutions.

The strongest interviews are not built around slogans. They are built around substance, experience, and the courage to say something useful instead of merely adding another cloud of noise to the national fog machine.
