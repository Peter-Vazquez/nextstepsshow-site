Guest Preparation

Before You Appear

Approved guests should be prepared to discuss their topic clearly, provide accurate background information, and participate in a thoughtful conversation suited for a live radio and podcast audience.

  • Use a quiet location with reliable phone or internet access.
  • Be ready before the scheduled call or appearance time.
  • Send any relevant links, bios, headshots, or media materials through the form.
  • Keep talking points clear, concise, and useful for listeners.
  • Remember that live show appearances are considered within the Monday-Friday, 12 PM-1 PM Eastern schedule.
