Guest Approval Process

How the Guest Process Works

Guest submissions are reviewed before any scheduling link is sent.

1

Submit the Guest Form

Complete the guest intake form and podcast guest release above.

2

Review

Your submission, topic, background, and availability are reviewed.

3

Approval

If approved, you will receive a private scheduling link by email.

4

Schedule

Approved guests may schedule a preparation call or approved appearance slot.

5

Prepare

Watch your email for confirmation details, topic notes, and guest preparation information.

Guest Preparation

Before You Appear

Approved guests should be prepared to discuss their topic clearly, provide accurate background information, and participate in a thoughtful conversation suited for a live radio and podcast audience.

  • Use a quiet location with reliable phone or internet access.
  • Be ready before the scheduled call or appearance time.
  • Send any relevant links, bios, headshots, or media materials through the form.
  • Keep talking points clear, concise, and useful for listeners.
  • Live show appearances are considered within the Monday-Friday, 12 PM-1 PM Eastern schedule.
