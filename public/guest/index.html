Be a Guest

Bring your story, message, or expertise to The Next Steps Show.

The Next Steps Show welcomes guests who have something meaningful to say, something useful to teach, or a story that can challenge, inform, and encourage listeners.

```

Conversations That Move People Forward

Hosted by Peter Vazquez, The Next Steps Show features conversations with leaders, entrepreneurs, pastors, authors, public officials, advocates, veterans, nonprofit leaders, business owners, and everyday people whose stories carry real insight.

The show is built around thoughtful discussion, practical wisdom, and honest engagement. Guests should be prepared to speak clearly about their work, their experience, their mission, and the next steps they believe listeners should consider.

Who Makes a Strong Guest?

A strong guest does not need to be famous. A strong guest needs to be prepared, honest, useful, and willing to have a real conversation. The best interviews help listeners better understand an issue, see a story from a new angle, or take practical action in their own lives.

If your work touches faith, leadership, business, culture, public policy, family, entrepreneurship, community service, personal transformation, or civic responsibility, your voice may be a strong fit for the program.

Guest Topics May Include

  • Faith, values, and personal transformation
  • Leadership and service
  • Business and entrepreneurship
  • Family, culture, and community life
  • Public policy and civic engagement
  • Books, campaigns, causes, and nonprofit work
  • Stories of resilience, recovery, and purpose
```

Before You Reach Out

Come prepared with a clear message.

The strongest guest pitches are focused, specific, and listener-centered. Tell us who you are, what topic you want to discuss, why it matters now, and what listeners will take away from the conversation.

```

Your Story

Share the experience, background, or mission that makes your perspective worth hearing.

Your Topic

Explain the issue, idea, book, organization, campaign, or cause you want to discuss.

Your Next Step

Be ready to give listeners a practical takeaway, challenge, or action step.

```
```

What to Send

To be considered as a guest, send a brief introduction with your name, organization or affiliation, proposed topic, relevant links, and why the conversation would be valuable for The Next Steps Show audience.

If you are representing a book, campaign, business, ministry, nonprofit, public office, media project, or advocacy effort, include the most useful background information so the show can evaluate the fit and prepare properly.

Include These Details

  • Name and contact information
  • Short bio or background
  • Proposed interview topic
  • Website or media links
  • Book, project, campaign, or organization details
  • Why the topic matters to listeners now
```

Guest Inquiry

Interested in joining the show?

Reach out with your guest idea, proposed topic, and background information. The Next Steps Show team will review the inquiry and follow up if it appears to be a good fit.
