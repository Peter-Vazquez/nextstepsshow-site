Our Mission

The Next Steps Show exists to move people from conversation to action.

Hosted by Peter Vazquez, The Next Steps Show brings together faith, leadership, business, culture, civic life, and real-world problem solving through conversations that challenge, inform, and encourage people to take their next step.

Putting People First

The heart of The Next Steps Show is simple: people matter, stories matter, and leadership matters. The program creates space for honest conversations about the issues shaping our families, communities, businesses, churches, and country.

Each episode is built around thoughtful discussion, practical insight, and the belief that listeners should not only understand what is happening around them, but also be encouraged to respond with clarity, courage, and purpose.

Faith, Leadership, Business, and Community

The show highlights guests and topics that speak to the full range of public and personal life. From entrepreneurs and pastors to public officials, advocates, authors, veterans, community leaders, and everyday people with meaningful stories, The Next Steps Show focuses on conversations that help listeners think deeper and act wiser.

The goal is not noise. The goal is movement. Better decisions. Stronger families. Healthier communities. More responsible leadership. A clearer understanding of the moment we are in.

What We Cover

  • Faith and values
  • Leadership and personal responsibility
  • Business, entrepreneurship, and work
  • Family, culture, and community life
  • Public policy and civic engagement
  • Stories of resilience, service, and transformation

Why It Matters

Real conversations for real people.

The Next Steps Show is built for listeners who are tired of shallow talking points and want conversations that connect ideas to everyday life. The show is serious when it needs to be, practical when it should be, and always focused on helping people move forward.

Inform

We help listeners understand the issues, stories, and decisions shaping their lives and communities.

Challenge

We ask direct questions, examine assumptions, and invite people to think beyond the easy headline.

Encourage

We point people toward purpose, responsibility, service, and practical next steps.
