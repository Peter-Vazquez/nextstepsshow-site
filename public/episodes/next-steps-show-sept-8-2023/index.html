Episode Details

This week, we're pleased to welcome Laureen Oliver, co-founder of the New York State Independence Party and its Chairwoman from 1993 to 2000. Outside of New York, she was instrumental in forming the national Reform Party. Widely recognized for her expertise in ballots, even Mayor Bloomberg once considered her for his presidential campaign team. Additionally, she chaired the Support Popular Vote from 2009 to 2012. Laureen is now a candidate for the Monroe County Republican Chair.

Listen in to learn what Next Steps one person has taken for the betterment of others.
