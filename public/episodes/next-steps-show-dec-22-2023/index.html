Next Steps Show Dec 22, 2023

Duration: 48:57

Today, we are joined by Marie Stroughter, a founding member of African American Conservatives, to discuss black and brown conservatism. This philosophy, rooted in self-sufficiency and traditionalism, has seen a shift from initial Republican support to a Democratic preference, a trend now slowly changing. We'll explore the global impact of black and brown conservatism, particularly its influence from Africa across the world, and discuss the evolving political tendencies within these communities. This conversation is more than mere dialogue; it's about fostering understanding, promoting growth, and discovering hope in challenging times. #NextStepsShow #PuttingPeopleFirst
