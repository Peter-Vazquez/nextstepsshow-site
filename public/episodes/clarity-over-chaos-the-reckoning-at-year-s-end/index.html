Clarity Over Chaos: The Reckoning at Year’s End

Duration: 49:09

The year closed with a reckoning. Money without math. Values without truth. Stefan Padfield, Executive Director of the Free Enterprise Project at the National Center for Public Policy Research, exposed how ESG burns trust while hiding behind virtue. Marie Fischer, Project 21 Ambassador, warned that comfort breeds surrender as youth drift and culture decays. Reverend Steven L. Craft, M.Div., Prison Chaplain &amp; Author, thundered that freedom dies without morality. Clarity over chaos. Accountability over optics. The fight continues into the new year.
