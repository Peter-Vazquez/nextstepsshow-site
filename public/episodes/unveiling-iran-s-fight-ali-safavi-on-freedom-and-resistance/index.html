Unveiling Iran's Fight: Ali Safavi on Freedom and Resistance

Duration: 24:06

Iran's Fight for Freedom: A Hard-Hitting Conversation with Ali Safavi Tune in to the latest episode of The Next Step Show on the Voice of Liberty for an explosive discussion with Ali Safavi, a leading voice in Iran's opposition in exile and member of the NCRI’s Foreign Affairs Committee. This isn’t just talk—it’s a wake-up call about a regime fueling global terrorism, crushing its people, and destabilizing the world from the Middle East to America’s backyard. Why You Need to Listen: Unmasking Iran’s Regime: Safavi exposes the brutal reality of a government executing 90 people in a single month-three per day-while masquerading as "moderate." A Cry for Freedom: Hear how Iran’s people are ready to rise, with resistance units defying the IRGC despite deadly crackdowns, and why change must come from within. Global Threat: From Venezuela to Cuba, Iran’s regime is exporting chaos, propping up dictators, and threatening U.S. security. Safavi reveals the chilling connections. A Vision for a Free Iran: The NCRI’s plan for a democratic, secular, non-nuclear Iran, with free elections and equality for all—Kurds, Arabs, Persians, Muslims, Jews, Christians, and more. No Kinetic Fix: Why airstrikes won’t topple this regime, and what Western governments must do to support Iran’s people without firing a shot. Key Moment: Safavi shares the personal tragedy of his brother’s execution by the regime, fueling his 40+ year fight for freedom. Tens of thousands rallied in Brussels on September 6th, joined by global heavyweights like Mike Pence and John Bercow, demanding action against Iran’s nuclear ambitions. Join the Fight for Truth: This episode is a rallying cry for liberty, exposing a regime’s corruption and incompetence while amplifying the Iranian people’s resolve. Don’t miss this hard-hitting conversation that cuts through the noise. Listen Now: Available on all major podcast platforms.
