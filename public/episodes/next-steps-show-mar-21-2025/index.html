Episode Details

Imagine a space where the air hums with the unfiltered pursuit of truth—a place where voices rise, not in echo, but in challenge. Our latest radio show invites you into this arena, where the stakes are nothing less than the world we share.

This isn’t about one story, one guest, or one fight. It’s about the enduring struggle to carve out a future that honors freedom, demands justice, and rewards courage.

Cybersecurity expert Jeff Minnis joins us to illuminate these themes. Picture a conversation that sweeps across the landscape of our lives—touching the unseen battles in the digital shadows, the systems that shape our daily existence, and the ideas that define who we are.

With every word, we peel back the layers of a world in flux, revealing the power each of us holds to shift its course. This episode is a spark. It’s for the curious, the restless, the determined—for anyone who believes that standing still is not an option.

Tune in, not just to listen, but to feel the weight of what’s possible when we dare to question, to act, and to stand as one.
