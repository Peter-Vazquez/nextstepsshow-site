Next Steps Show July 22, 2024

Duration: 57:12

In today’s episode of The Next Steps Show, we sit down with Septimus A. Scott, a relentless advocate, U.S. Army veteran, and accomplished leader in healthcare administration. With a deep commitment to improving mental health outcomes and uplifting communities, Septimus brings a wealth of knowledge and experience to the conversation. From his work in Cognitive Behavioral Therapy and evidence-based practices to his leadership in gun related violence prevention initiatives, Septimus is making a real impact. We discuss his journey, including how his walk with Jesus has influenced his life and work, his thoughts on the current political climate, and the role of faith and leadership in navigating today’s challenges. Do not miss this powerful and insightful discussion with a man who is transforming lives and empowering communities. #PuttingPeopleFirst
