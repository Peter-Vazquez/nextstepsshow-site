Next Steps Show June 2, 2023

Duration: 55:15

Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to another inspiring episode of the Next Steps Show. Today, we have a guest who personifies the very spirit of courage, resilience, and unwavering dedication to our nation. A stalwart Army veteran, a fierce advocate for freedom, and a firm leader, he embodies the qualities that make a true American. He's a hero, not just on the battlefield, but also in the courtroom and corridors of power. He bravely stood up against forces larger than life, blowing the whistle on matters of grave importance that involved Hillary Clinton, the Clinton Foundation, and the controversial Uranium One sale to Russia. He dared to question, to expose, to uphold truth and integrity. Moreover, his commitment to safeguarding American democracy has taken him on a new path, a journey into the political arena. Today, he's not just a whistleblower, but also a promising candidate for the US House of Representatives for West Virginia's 2nd Congressional District. Ladies and gentlemen, we are truly honored and privileged to have on our show, a man who has displayed exceptional courage, and an unwavering commitment to his oath to protect the United States against all threats - both foreign and domestic. Please join me in welcoming the honorable Nate Cain (NateCain4WV.com).
