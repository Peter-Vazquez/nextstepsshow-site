Next Steps Show April 10, 2025

Duration: 49:08

Where do we draw the line between being a welcoming nation and a nation being walked over? In this hard-hitting episode of The Next Steps Show, guest host Luis Martinez doesn’t hold back. With firsthand experience as a former green card holder turned proud U.S. citizen, he unpacks the real meaning of legal immigration, assimilation, and American values. He exposes how media spin, activist judges, and political cowardice are eroding our sovereignty — replacing law and order with toxic empathy and willful ignorance. Why are sanctuary cities allowed to defy federal law? Why are victims of illegal alien crime ignored while violators are coddled? Why do legal immigrants face endless red tape while illegals are fast-tracked to benefits and voting access? These are the uncomfortable questions this show confronts head-on. Luis also challenges the double standard in our justice system, spotlighting federal judges like James Boasberg whose personal conflicts of interest muddy the rule of law — reaching beyond their jurisdictions to block the will of the American people while conveniently ignoring blatant violations happening in their own backyards. And finally, a powerful reminder: this nation has a culture worth protecting. Assimilation is not oppression. It’s the foundation of unity. Without it, multiculturalism becomes division masked as inclusion. This is more than commentary — it’s a call to vigilance. A defense of the rule of law. A reminder that the American Dream is a privilege, not a free-for-all. Listen to this episode and ask yourself: Are we preserving America, or passively surrendering it?
