Episode Details

Join Peter Vazquez and Ayesha Kreutz for a great conversation with Armando Marianetti, a small business owner, educator, Finger Lakes Region representative for fairtax.org and volunteers at ConventionofStates.com, AuditNY.com and stands at the ready to help anyone interested in protecting honest elections, fair taxation, and our constitution.

---

Armando Marianetti has been a small business owner since 1978, including ventures in the restaurant industry, retail, financial growth services, and college/adult tutoring. From East Rochester, NY - Mr. Marianetti earned his Bachelors’ degree in Government from Georgetown University then a Masters’ degree in Education from Roberts Wesleyan College. Armando Marianetti is a graduate of East Rochester High School where he earned acceptance into the National Honor Society. Armando served as a US & Global History teacher to special education students at Dr. Freddie Thomas High School in the Rochester City School District.
