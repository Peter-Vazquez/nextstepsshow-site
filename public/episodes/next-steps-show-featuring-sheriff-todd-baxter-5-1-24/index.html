Next Steps Show Featuring Sheriff Todd Baxter 5-1-24

Duration: 49:01

Join us as we welcome the Honorable Sheriff Todd Baxter, a 36-year law enforcement veteran and Monroe County's 49th Sheriff. With a distinguished background that includes service in the Rochester Police Department, the U.S. Army, and as Chief of Police in the Town of Greece, NY, Sheriff Baxter is a champion of ethical leadership and veteran support. On this episode of the Next Steps Show, he will share his insights on the state of Monroe County from a law enforcement perspective. Be a leader. Share your story.
