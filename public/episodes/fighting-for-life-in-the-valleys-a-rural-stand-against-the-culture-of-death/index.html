Fighting for Life in the Valleys: A Rural Stand Against the Culture of Death

Duration: 48:50

In an America that too often trades conviction for convenience, two rural warriors rise up to defend the unborn. Charlene Reddy and Connie Nichols of the Pregnancy Resource Center of the Valleys are not waiting for government handouts or political permission. They are doing the work—on the ground, in the trenches—standing up for life, truth, and real healing in a state hellbent on silencing them. This is not theory. This is not policy wonkery. This is the real-world impact of faith in action. They share gut-wrenching stories: women carrying decades of post-abortive pain, men broken by the loss of children they never got to hold, and entire communities gaslit into believing death is a right and life is an inconvenience. You’ll hear how PRC Valleys operates completely free of government funding—because they refuse to compromise their Christ-centered mission. You’ll hear how they offer real options, real counseling, real fatherhood programs, and healing ministries that Planned Parenthood never will. And you’ll hear the truth: that the abortion industry isn’t about empowerment—it’s about erasure. Erasing faith, erasing family, and erasing the future. If you're tired of the lies, if you're hungry for truth, and if you're ready to take the next step—this is your call to action. Listen now.
