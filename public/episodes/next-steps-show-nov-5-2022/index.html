Join Peter Vazquez and Ayesha Kreutz for a great conversation with New York Citizens Audit (NYCA) – Marly Hornik, founding member and Director &amp; Kim Hermance, founding member and Trustee. --- New York Citizens Audit (NYCA) is a non–partisan team of concerned citizen volunteers working to restore election integrity to the Empire State. These heroes have volunteered thousands of hours to do what the state will not do - audit the New York State voter database. Using publicly available data, NYCA has analyzed over 20 million records in the New York State Voter Rolls, finding millions of irregular registrations that impacted the results of every race in the 2020 General Election. NYCA believes an end-to-end audit of the NYS Board of Elections is warranted. Armed with the facts, our volunteer County Strike Teams bring NYCA’s documented evidence to officials. We intend to pursue every remedy available under NYS Law and the Constitution to bring accountability for the Citizens of New York State. Marly Hornik: Marly is the Founder and Director of the non-partisan, grassroots organization New York Citizens Audit (NYCA). She leads a team of hundreds of citizen professionals from across the state who investigate, research, and audit the New York State Voter Rolls. Marly has been a speaker and advocate for election integrity in New York State and across the nation. Kim Hermance: Kim is a founding member and a Trustee of New York Citizens Audit (NYCA). Kim serves as the statewide volunteer coordinator and organizer of NYCA‘s County Strike teams. Kim believes citizens need to understand what happened in the 2020 election so the systemic issues in the election process can be fixed for future generations. She has worked tirelessly in NY State to educate citizens about election integrity. For more information, or to join the audit teams, or to report something you found just a little off when voting go to - AuditNY.com. --- NextStepsShow.com wysl1040.com
