Episode Details

In this episode, we take a hard look at leadership and accountability, revisiting a pivotal conversation from the archives. Reporting live from the 2024 Republican National Convention, Paul Ciminelli breaks down key updates, including Senator JD Vance of Ohio being considered as a potential vice-presidential pick for former President Trump. We also delve into the ongoing criminal investigation involving the Monroe County Clerk’s Office. This discussion highlights significant failures under the leadership of current County Clerk Jamie Romeo, including: A lack of transparency and delayed action when the Office became aware of theft involving 16 individuals. Concerns about altered and stolen documents that have impacted our community. A pattern of missed opportunities to address trends and safeguard the public. This is a vital episode underscoring how mismanagement affects our community and what must be done to ensure better leadership moving forward. #PuttingPeopleFirst
