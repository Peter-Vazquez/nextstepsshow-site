What if everything you thought was secure—your vote, your water, your very way of life—was slipping through your fingers? We expose two of the most critical crises facing our nation: the silent war on clean water and the battle for election integrity. These are not distant problems. They are happening right now, in your community, in your home. Millions of Americans unknowingly consume toxic water, contaminated by PFAS chemicals and industrial waste. Indigenous communities are denied access, forced to travel miles for what should be a basic human right. And across the world, people drink from filthy rivers because they have no choice. One man decided to change that. Christopher Beth, founder of The Bucket Ministry, joins us to share how a single mission trip to the Brazilian Amazon opened his eyes—and how his organization is bringing clean, safe water to those who need it most. But as America’s water crisis grows, will we fight for those suffering right here at home? If you cannot trust your vote, can you trust anything? Ballot tampering, voter fraud, and new laws like the SAVE Act are setting the stage for a battle over election integrity. Is proof of citizenship the safeguard we need, or a barrier to democracy? Is our system broken beyond repair? From the Motor Voter Act to legal battles over ballot security, we expose the fight happening in real-time—a fight that will define our nation’s future. Two battles. One night. One truth: If we do not fight for justice now, we may not have the chance later. Will you listen, or will you look away? Catch the podcast: NextStepsShow.com or wysl1040.com.
