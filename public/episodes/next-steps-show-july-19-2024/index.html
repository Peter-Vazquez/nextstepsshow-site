Next Steps Show July 19, 2024

Duration: 55:58

In this episode of The Next Steps Show, we explore what it means to lead with purpose, adapt in challenging times, and stay grounded in values that truly matter. Our guest, Hon. Señora Meg Allocco-Angell, has a story that resonates deeply. From her early days in education to her journey as an integrative health practitioner and homeschooling mom, her life has been shaped by faith, resilience, and a commitment to empowering others. She opens up about her walk with Christ, the decision to homeschool her children, and how she navigates the complexities of today’s political and social climate. We also delve into the ripple effects of the recent Microsoft outage that brought Monroe County to a standstill. It is a reminder of how reliant we have become on technology and a call to consider how we prepare for the unexpected. Through it all, this episode invites you to reflect on the importance of community, faith, and leadership. It is about finding strength in adversity and taking that next step forward—no matter the obstacles. #PuttingPeopleFIrst
