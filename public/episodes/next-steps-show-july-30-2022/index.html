Next Steps Show July 30, 2022

Duration: 55:15

Join our conversation with Septimus Scott and Ryan James Turner as we explore the Rochester Works Gun Violence Prevention Program. This initiative aims to offer job training, credentialing, and job placement in well-paying roles for around 675 young adults, aged 18-24, who are unemployed, underemployed, or out of school, primarily targeting certain areas within Monroe County. We'll discuss the program's approach in creating networks with small community-based and faith-based organizations to extend its reach to youths who are often overlooked by conventional methods. Additionally, we'll delve into how these young adults are supported beyond job opportunities, receiving assistance in areas like technical skills, childcare, and monetary incentives, as well as access to essential services such as food and housing. The program also connects them to various grant-funded training programs, aiming to enhance their career paths and future prospects. This conversation is more than mere dialogue; it's about fostering understanding, promoting growth, and discovering hope in challenging times. #NextStepsShow #PuttingPeopleFirst
