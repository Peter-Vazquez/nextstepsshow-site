Episode Details

The media twisted the story, but today, we set the record straight. Jeff Briggs called out the absurdity of ‘furries’ in schools, and for that, he was smeared as a racist. His message was simple: schools should focus on education, not indulging delusions. But the left, desperate to control the narrative, seized on his gorilla suit protest to drown out the real issue—our kids’ classrooms are becoming ideological battlegrounds.

We are also breaking down Rochester’s city council race, exposing the myth of income inequality, and asking: how much more affordable housing do we really need? Plus, what comes after DEI, and why are thousands marching against Trump and Musk?

The truth is under attack, but we are not backing down. Listen now. #talkradio
