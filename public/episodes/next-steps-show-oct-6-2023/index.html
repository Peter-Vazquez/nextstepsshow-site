This week, we are thrilled to welcome Darryl Anderson, the devoted Podcast Host and Producer of Man2Man360. His aim is to empower men through conversations deeply rooted in a biblical worldview. Alongside his 'All Stars' guest hosts on the podcast, Darryl inspires and encourages men to navigate life's journey with strength, wisdom, and the uplifting power of Jesus Christ.

Join us for an enlightening conversation, infused with hope, resilience, and the transformative power of community.
