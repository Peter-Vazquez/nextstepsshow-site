Next Steps Show May 12, 2023

Duration: 55:12

This week on the Next Steps Show, we are honored to introduce a distinguished member of our community, a man of unwavering faith and a lifelong commitment to Christianity. As a devoted father of three boys, he has dedicated his life to altruistic endeavors and fostering a positive impact on the lives of others. Our esteemed guest's exceptional service to his country is exemplified through his tenure in the Navy. Furthermore, he has actively engaged with various volunteer organizations, including The Good News Jail &amp; Prison Ministry and Youth for Christ. Additionally, he selflessly serves as a Police Chaplain for The Town of Gates Police Department, offering spiritual support to law enforcement officers and their families. We are privileged to welcome the Founder and Executive Director of Coffee for Cops, an organization dedicated to fostering positive relationships between law enforcement and the communities they serve. Through his unwavering commitment to these noble causes, he has garnered immense respect and admiration from his peers and community members alike. Please join us in extending a warm welcome to the Honorable Michael Barbato.
