Next Steps Show Nov 17, 2023

Duration: 49:02

Join us in an eye-opening dialogue as we sit down with Pastor Tamara Marcella, a dynamic voice in today’s challenging world. We're diving deep into topics that often stir up one-sided debates or are shrouded in ignorance. Let's bring them into the light! Today we’re talking: Crime &amp; Hopelessness in the City: Exploring the roots and solutions. Women as Pastors: Is it Biblical or not? Delving into scriptures like 1 Timothy 2:12, Titus 1, 1 Timothy 3, and the roles of biblical figures like Phoebe and Priscilla. Politics &amp; the Church: Should the church be politically active? Weighing scriptures like Matthew 22:21 and Micah 6:8. In an era where faith is tested, businesses grapple with regulations, and leaders dare to stand firm in their beliefs. It's time to challenge the status quo, both in society and within the church itself. It's more than just a talk; it's about understanding, growth, and finding hope in tough times. #NextStepsShow #PuttingPeopleFirst #WYSL1040 NextStepsShow.com wysl1040.com
