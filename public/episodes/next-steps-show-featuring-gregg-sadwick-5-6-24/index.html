Next Steps Show Featuring Gregg Sadwick 5-6-24

Duration: 49:01

On this episode, we welcome Gregg Sadwick to the Next Steps Show. A Navy veteran, local entrepreneur, and Rochester native, he is deeply rooted in Monroe County and strives for reform through bipartisan solutions and fiscal responsibility. Our guest champions tackling inflation, prioritizing vetting in immigration, and advocating for housing affordability. He opposes punitive measures and emphasizes comprehensive solutions. In foreign policy, he stresses strength, alliances, and accountability. Gregg Sadwick's vision includes term limits and transparency to restore faith in the NYS 25th Congressional District.
