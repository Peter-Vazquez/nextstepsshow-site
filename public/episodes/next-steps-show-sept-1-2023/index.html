Today's guest, Larry Staub, has dedicated over three decades to serving in local government. He is a former Chief of Staff for Monroe County's Republican Legislative Majority and has been a pivotal figure in major campaign efforts and redistricting. Beyond his commendable public service, Larry is an honored Eagle Scout, recognized for his heroism. With a lifetime of leadership and community service under his belt, he is now campaigning for the role of Chairman of the Monroe County Republican Party. Tune in for an insightful conversation with the candidate for Monroe County Republican Party Chairman.
