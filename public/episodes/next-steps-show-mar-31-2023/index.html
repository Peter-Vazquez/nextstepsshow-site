This week on the Next Steps Show, we are excited to welcome an inspiring individual to our program. Raised in Massapequa, NY and now a proud resident of Rochester for 25 years, he has made a significant impact on his community. He and his wife, Kim, have raised four children in Brighton, NY, where he is an active member of the community. As the Leader of the Brighton Republican Committee, he is a respected voice for bringing balance to our political system. He is known for his unwavering dedication and exceptional leadership skills, which have earned him a reputation as an outstanding pillar of our society. Please welcome Patrick Reilly (Pat). Pat is a telecommunications executive and a lifelong New Yorker with conservative Republican values. In addition to his role in Brighton, Pat has served as Executive Vice-Chairman for the Monroe County Republican Committee, is active in the Gun Owners of America, and volunteers for the Frederick Douglass Foundation of NY. He is also a candidate for office and has received endorsements from both the Republican and Conservative Parties in his race for Monroe County Legislature in the 14th District.
