Episode Details

Today on the Next Steps Show, we are thrilled to welcome Nancy Lewis, a devoted Registered Nurse, lifelong Pittsford resident, and candidate for Monroe County Legislature, District 10. With over a decade of committed community service under her belt, Nancy is deeply dedicated to promoting economic growth and ensuring public safety. Her efforts are not confined to Pittsford and Brighton alone; they extend across the entirety of Monroe County. A proud mother of five, her vision for a thriving, safe community is both a professional aspiration and a deeply personal endeavor. To learn more, please visit votenancylewis.com.

Listen in to learn what Next Steps one person has taken for the betterment of others.
