Next Steps Show Sept 22, 2023

Duration: 55:14

This week, our guest is a seasoned telecommunications executive who has been deeply committed to serving the Town of Brighton. Since 2021, he has held the role of Town Leader for the Brighton Republican Committee. A conservative Republican, he has called Brighton home for over 23 years, where he and his wife, Kim, have proudly raised their four children. He is actively involved with the Gun Owners of America and dedicates his time as a volunteer for the Frederick Douglass Foundation of NY. In addition to his leadership in Brighton, our guest has served as the Executive Vice-Chairman for the Monroe County Republican Committee. He is currently campaigning for both the Chairman seat of the Monroe County Republican Committee and the Monroe County Leg Dist. 14 position. Welcome Pat Reilly, Candidate for the Monroe County Republican Committee Chairman seat.
