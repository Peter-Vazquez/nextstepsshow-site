Episode Details

Power unchecked. Corruption exposed. Leadership tested.

A former Greece official prepares to plead guilty—what does this mean for local accountability? Tensions explode in Irondequoit as the town board confronts the fallout of the Andraé Evans investigation.

Meanwhile, in New York City, Mayor Eric Adams, the DOJ, and Andrew Cuomo are caught in a political storm that reeks of backroom deals and power plays.

And now, as state corrections officers strike, Governor Hochul is ready to deploy the National Guard. Overworked officers, unsafe prisons, families in crisis—who pays the price for failed leadership?

Truth is under siege, and power protects its own. But real leadership does not flinch in the face of corruption. It stands up. It speaks out. It demands better.
