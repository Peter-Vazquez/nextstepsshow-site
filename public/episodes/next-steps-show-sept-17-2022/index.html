Join Peter & Ayesha on the Next Step Show for a great conversation with Orlando Rivera, Candidate for the 136th NYS Assembly District.

-------

Orlando Rivera is the first Afro-Latino to run the NYS Assembly in the 136th district. He is the son of a single mother who worked hard to provide for him and his siblings. A proud graduate of Bishop Kearney High School in Irondequoit, and the State University of NY at Potsdam.

Orlando is an experienced non-profit professional who’s focused on the value of service to consumers and assisting others for the overall betterment of our community.

Mr. Rivera and his family reside in the historic Maplewood neighborhood (Rochester, NY)

Orlando Rivera is the Republican/ Conservative candidate for the 136th Assembly District.
