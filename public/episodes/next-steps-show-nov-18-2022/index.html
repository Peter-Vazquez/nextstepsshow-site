Join Peter Vazquez and Ayesha Kreutz for a great conversation with Rodney Brown Journalist, researcher, lecturer (youth self-empowerment), and author. --- Rodney Brown is a graduate of Claflin University in Orangeburg, South Carolina, where he doubled majored in both Mass Communication and Sociology with a minor in Political Science. His educational development extends to Cornell University, where he studied for an abbreviated period under James E. Turner, professor emeritus of African American Politics and Social Policy and founding Director of the Dr. John H. Clarke Africana Studies and Research Center. Mr. Brown currently lives in Rochester, New York. He's also the Executive Director Brown Publishing LLC, a multimedia outlet which is geared to disseminate news and resourceful information through several platforms including (Print- Southwest Tribune), Digital-internet), and Radio- The Crow’s Nest Radio Show, and soon to be Television. Brown Publishing LLC founding is based on our responsibility to rekindle journalism and restore trust, integrity, and accountability in the media. Our mission seeks to present a balanced view of relative issues to build bridges among diverse populations. southwesttribune.com --- NextStepsShow.com wysl1040.com
