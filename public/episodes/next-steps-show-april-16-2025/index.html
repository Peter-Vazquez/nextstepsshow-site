The Next Steps Show: Voice of Liberty in a Time of Chaos This isn’t your average midday talk. It’s raw, it’s real and it’s unapologetically driven by truth. Peter Vazquez steps up to the mic with a message that cuts through the noise: we are being lied to, taxed into submission, and told that our values are outdated. From the founding of federal income tax to the gutting of Title IX, Peter connects the dots past to present to show how today’s policies are shredding the Constitution and betraying the American people. He calls out Gavin “Newsom Gruesome,” drags New York’s "Hocus Pocus" leadership, and tears the mask off the victimhood narrative being force-fed to minorities and working families. Peter doesn’t hide behind party lines he challenges everyone, Democrat or Republican, to defend what they believe or get out of the way. It’s about civic literacy, not political theater. It’s about real Americans losing real freedoms while D.C. and statehouses run cover for confusion and cowardice. You’ll hear stories that matter, like the makeshift homeless "living rooms" under California overpasses, and why Title IX isn't about “inclusion” it’s about integrity. Peter shares biblical truth that lifts up, even as he exposes darkness. “Have nothing to do with fruitless deeds but rather expose them.” That’s Ephesians 5:11—and that’s the mission of this show. The Next Steps Show is not just conversation it’s confrontation with conviction. If you’re tired of dancing around the truth, tune in and meet up. Call in. Speak up.
