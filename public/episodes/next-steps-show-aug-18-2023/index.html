Next Steps Show Aug 18, 2023

Duration: 55:14

Today, we journey down a path with a remarkable individual who has triumphed over some of the most daunting challenges one can face in life. Emerging as a fervent advocate for God, Country, and Family, she shares her story to inspire, uplift, and foster collaboration. Friends, please join me in welcoming to the Next Step Show – the Honorable, the indomitable force of spirit, the Mayor of Realville, Ayesha Kreutz. Not only is she the founder of the FDFNY and the FDF National State Coordinator, but she is also the cherished co-host of the Next Steps Show and my dear friend. Like the Genie from Disney's Aladdin once said, ' You'd be surprised what you can live through .' Ayesha is a testament to the resilience and strength of the human spirit.
