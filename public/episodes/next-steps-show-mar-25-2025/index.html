Episode Details

While we honor the heroes who earned the Medal of Honor with blood and grit, another battle rages here at home—one not fought with rifles, but with gavels, manipulation, and political lawfare. The judiciary is no longer guarding the Constitution—it is being used to dismantle it. Sixty-four nationwide injunctions were fired at President Trump, a sharp increase from the 20 faced by the Obama administration over eight years. Not because of law, but because of politics. That is not justice—it is sabotage. Marcus Carey joins the conversation. A constitutional warrior with 45 years in the trenches, he took on the judicial machine and won. From shutting down unconstitutional speech bans to exposing the rigged game of judge-shopping—where plaintiffs strategically file lawsuits in districts with judges more likely to rule in their favor, as seen in the travel ban cases—and shadow rulings, Marcus brings the truth the Left does not want you to hear. We dig deep into how power is being hijacked from the presidency, how courts are being weaponized, and why New York’s policy on involuntary mental health removals is just another mask for government overreach. Critics argue that this policy lacks sufficient oversight and could lead to abuses, raising concerns about the balance between public safety and individual rights. This is not debate. It is defense—of our values, our laws, and our nation. Hit play. Stand firm. The Republic needs you alert.
