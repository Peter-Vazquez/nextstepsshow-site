Episode Details

This week our conversation included Earl Wallace, Pastor, Patriot, Author, Teacher & Musician, 3BR & LCF.

Earl is an American Patriot, who has served in the US Army, has been a high school English teacher, a Christian musician, a Bible conference teacher, a veterans disability claims representative, a NY Statewide Veterans Program Administrator, and a Tea Party spokesperson throughout UpState, NY, which included TV and radio appearances. He also is the author of “The Three-Dimensional Leader: Negotiating Your Mission, Resources and Context,” which he has taught throughout America, in Europe, Asia and The Middle East. He now is recognized widely as Pastor Earl, founding pastor of Liberty Christian Fellowship Church, in the greater Capital District of NYS. His booklet titled, "The Bible Is The Source of America's Inalienable Rights”is gaining national attention to help people understand America’s true founding, and how we can become the kind of people whom God can use once again to restore our beloved USA!

In 2012, while teaching a 3D Leadership course in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, he had two lunches and dinners with a self-identified jihadist, who advised him “You Americans are doomed, because you don’t know how to defend your culture. We will use every freedom against you!” From that encounter Earl realized he must overcome presuppositions and general ideas about “God, mom and apple pie” to determine specifically how America was founded as a Christian nation, and what must take place to defend it. The answer is what Earl calls “The Biblical Basis of the Bill of Rights.” Earl discovered how America’s Biblical foundation is being undermined; who is doing it; how they are doing it, and the revival of repentance and reformation that can overcome it with Christian discipleship that applies the Bible to every aspect of life, including civics!

In 2014, Earl became the founding pastor of Liberty Christian Fellowship Church in the Capital District of NY to teach how the Bible applies to every aspect of life, including civics. “Pastor Earl” speaks at events and conferences to teach “The Biblical Basis of The Bill of Rights” to call Americans to reaffirm our Biblical, Godgiven, Ten Commandments, natural, unalienable rights, upon which American culture was founded. He also teaches “The Politics That Pollute Paradise” to explain how specifically America’s Biblical Foundation is being undermined. Earl’s program, titled “Prophets, Patriots and Providence” explains how God can revive America’s civics through our specific obedience to return to our nation’s Puritan roots and founding values and principles.

Earl says, “Without people understanding how to connect the dots between the Bible and America’s Founding documents, most of our patriotic efforts amount to a hollow cry of “Rah-rah, we need more God in government,” and few really know what that means. So we mistakenly think all religions have the same social, cultural and political outcomes, which they don’t! America’s founding documents applied Biblical truths to form our nation’s civics! Earl says, he can show us what we must do to re-covenant with God, as did our nation’s founding generations, who pledged to honor God in the Mayflower Compact, The Declaration of Independence and the US Bill of Rights!

Pastor Earl’s motto is “for Christ and Country,” and he has taught “The Biblical Basis of The Bill of Rights” to town boards, Christian conferences, civic groups, law enforcement agencies and at political meetings and 2 events to call Americans to reestablish the Puritan faith and ideals through which God worked to found our beloved USA!
