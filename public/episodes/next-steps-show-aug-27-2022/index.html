Episode Details

What do you get when a veteran Hollywood actor refuses to bow to the woke mob and decides instead to speak truth with boldness? You get Nick Searcy—a man who’s traded red carpets for real conversations about the soul of this nation.

Nick didn’t come on the show to talk fluff. We dove deep into what’s really at stake in America—our freedom to speak, to worship, to lead, and to live without being crushed under the weight of government overreach and media manipulation.

From Justified to Capitol Punishment, Nick has seen the inside of the entertainment machine—and he’s not afraid to expose it.

We talked about the cultural collapse happening around us, the loss of leadership in both Hollywood and government, and the need for Americans—especially believers and patriots—to stand firm and reclaim what’s been hijacked.

Leadership, as we unpacked, isn’t about titles or talking points. It’s about service, vision, and the courage to challenge broken systems, whether you're on set or on the campaign trail.

We even brought it back home—back to faith, back to family, and back to a God who still redeems broken lives, from the crack house to the pulpit.

This isn’t just another interview. It’s a conversation for those who are tired of the noise, hungry for truth, and ready to lead.

Listen, reflect, and take the next step. America still has a fighting chance—but only if her people are willing to lead.
