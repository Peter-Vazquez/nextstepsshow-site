Exposing the Ideological War on Life in New York

Duration: 49:06

What if the real war isn’t overseas—but in the womb, the classroom, and the courtroom? In this explosive episode of The Next Steps Show, Rev. Jim Harden of CompassCare delivers a relentless indictment of America’s ideological drift—from firebombed pregnancy centers to black-market population control. We expose the truth behind New York’s abortion machine, Planned Parenthood’s eugenic legacy, and the Biden administration’s weaponized silence. This isn’t just about policy. It’s about power—who gets to live, and who gets erased. What’s happening in New York is a case study in what happens when a government forgets God and turns on its own people. Rev. Harden brings the facts, the fire, and the faith. Listen in. Share widely. Stand firm.
