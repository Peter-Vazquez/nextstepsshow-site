Episode Details

In today’s episode of The Next Steps Show, we shine a light on the incredible work of Hon. David Lippa—a man who has dedicated his time, resources, and heart to saving lives and supporting families during their most challenging moments.

David shares his journey, his mission, and the driving force behind Stop the 22 a Day, an initiative aimed at addressing the devastating reality of veteran suicides. Through powerful stories and heartfelt conversations, he brings attention to the struggles many veterans face and the critical importance of offering hope and support.

We discuss David’s vision for the future, the impact of his work, and how individuals and communities can get involved in making a difference. For those who need support, veterans can access the Veterans Crisis Line by dialing 988 or 1-800-273-8255 and selecting option 1, texting 838255, or visiting VeteransCrisisLine.net.

This episode is a testament to the power of compassion and determination in changing lives. Do not miss this inspiring and impactful conversation with Hon. David Lippa.
