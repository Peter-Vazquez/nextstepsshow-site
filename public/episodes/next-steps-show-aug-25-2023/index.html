Next Steps Show Aug 25, 2023

Duration: 55:14

Today, our guest is a man whose intellectual journey has spanned diverse ideologies and disciplines. A former Marxist, he has evolved into an unwavering champion of liberty, combating forms of tyranny from socialism-communism to "woke" ideology. With a Ph.D. in Literary and Cultural Studies from Carnegie Mellon, he boasts an academic career that took him from NYU to Duke, Carnegie Mellon, and Hillsdale College, where he is a distinguished fellow. The author of twelve books—ranging from academic memoirs to incisive cultural critiques like "The Great Reset and the Struggle for Liberty"—our guest's words resonate not just in academic circles but also among general readers. Featured in prominent media outlets like The Washington Post, The Epoch Times, and Quillette, as well as political talk shows such as Tucker Carlson Tonight and Unfiltered with Dan Bongino. His voice reaches across lecture halls, conferences, and airwaves, consistently advocating for intellectual rigor and freedom. In an age rife with ideological polarization, our guest stands as a nuanced thinker unafraid to challenge prevailing narratives, reflecting a lifetime committed to the pursuit of truth and liberty – Dr. Michael Rectenwald.
