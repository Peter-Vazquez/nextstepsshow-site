Episode Details

We delve into the dark underbelly of politics, where emotional intelligence becomes a weapon, and manipulation is the name of the game. Leaders’ adept at reading emotions can inspire and unite, but when that skill morphs into trickery, the consequences can be fatal.

We explore how cunning politicians exploit emotional intelligence to deceive and control, leading to policies that cost lives. This isn't just political theater—it's a deadly game where real people pay the ultimate price.

Tune in as we uncover the tactics of these political puppeteers and discuss the human toll of their manipulations. It's time to pull back the curtain and confront the harsh realities of power and deceit.
