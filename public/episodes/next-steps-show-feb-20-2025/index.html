Next Steps Show Feb 20, 2025

Duration: 48:44

A government that abandons its own citizens is no government at all. Today, we expose the truth. Illegal immigrants handed Social Security numbers, benefits, and even passports—while American families struggle to make ends meet. A whistleblower comes forward, revealing how officials were pressured to qualify them for long-term disability at taxpayer expense. New York’s correction officers are under siege—forced into dangerous prisons with no protection, no backup, and no leadership willing to fight for them. The state’s response? Deploying untrained parole officers and National Guard troops to cover their failures while ignoring the real crisis. And in Rochester, activists push to remove police from 911 calls, replacing them with social workers—because apparently, crime is just a misunderstanding. The same people who wanted to defund the police are now quietly making it happen. Where does this end? A nation cannot survive if its leaders betray those who built it. We are watching the erosion of law, order, and accountability in real time. Enough! We pull back the curtain on corruption, the deception, and the policies that are dismantling our country piece by piece. No spin. No censorship. Just the truth.
