A Republic in Crisis — or Just in Denial? This episode dives deep into the unraveling of American institutions—from media manipulation to political cowardice to a culture that’s forgotten how to think critically, let alone civically. With raw honesty and a faith-based lens, Peter reflects on legacy, leadership, and loss—starting with his own growing family and a heartfelt nod to Parkinson’s Awareness. But it doesn’t stop at the personal. The conversation drills into the national crisis. From race-baiting politics to media lies, from the misuse of asylum claims to a mayor more interested in blocking reporters than blocking crime—this episode pulls no punches. It challenges false narratives about democracy, immigration, and justice, while exposing the cultural rot trickling down from activist judges and bureaucrats to broken communities. This isn't just talk radio. This is civic revival, truth-telling, and a call to spiritual and political clarity in the heart of America’s Van-bōōl-zal-ness Crisis. Listen now.
