Next Steps Show Dec 2, 2024

Duration: 53:01

In today’s episode of The Next Steps Show , we are joined by Marcus C. Williams, a prominent figure in Rochester, NY, for a powerful discussion about local politics, culture, and the importance of holding fast to dreams in the face of challenges. Anchored by Langston Hughes’ inspiring words, “Hold fast to dreams, for if dreams die, life is a broken-winged bird that cannot fly,” we delve into the intersection of faith, resilience, and leadership in uncertain times. We explore the meaning of Thanksgiving—both personal and historical—and reflect on its significance through biblical and cultural lenses. Together, Marcus and I tackle the pressing topics of today: from the controversy surrounding the Presidential pardon of Hunter Biden to the appearance of political symbols in unexpected places like Pittsford, NY. These moments challenge us to consider the deeper implications of our political and cultural landscape. This episode is a thought-provoking journey into the power of dreams, faith, and perseverance in shaping our personal and collective futures. Marcus brings a unique perspective to the conversation, making this an episode you will not want to miss. Join us as we speak candidly, reflect deeply, and look ahead to the next steps we can all take. #PuttingPeopleFirst
