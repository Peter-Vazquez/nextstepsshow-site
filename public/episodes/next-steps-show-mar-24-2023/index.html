Next Steps Show Mar 24, 2023

Duration: 55:15

This week on the Next Steps Show, we are thrilled to have another inspiring young adult join us. Daughter of the Next Steps Show, Co-Host Ayesha Kreutz, Kezia Latin. A courageous individual who has faced her own unique set of challenges and continues to strive for balance as she pursues success in faith, politics, and entrepreneurship. At just 24 years old, Kezia is already a mother of two and the owner/director of Elevation Dance in Churchville, NY. She holds a B.S. in Dance and a B.A. in Community Youth Development, both from Nazareth College. Kezia attributes her achievements to hard work, dedication, perseverance, strong interpersonal relationships, and her unyielding faith in Christ. As the scripture from Galatians 6:7 reminds us, "Do not be deceived: God cannot be mocked. A man reaps what he sows." Kezia embodies this message, and her story will undoubtedly inspire our listeners. Join us as we welcome the honorable Kezia Latin to the Next Steps Show.
