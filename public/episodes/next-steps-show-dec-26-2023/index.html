Next Steps Show Dec 26, 2023

Duration: 49:14

In today's discussion, we're focusing on several important decisions by New York Governor Kathy Hochul. These include her vetoes on bills about noncompete agreements and post-conviction appeals, the signing of the LLC Transparency Act, the formation of a reparations commission, and the change in local election timing. Each of these topics highlights key issues in New York's business, legal, and social spheres, and we'll explore their impacts and the debates surrounding them. An endeavor to deepen understanding, encourage personal and communal growth, and uncover hope amidst difficult times. #NextStepsShow #PuttingPeopleFirst
