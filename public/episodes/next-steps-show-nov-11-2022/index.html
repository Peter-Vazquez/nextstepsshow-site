Duration: 55:12

Join Peter Vazquez and Ayesha Kreutz for a great conversation with Bishop Dean Nelson, Executive Director for Human Coalition Action, Chairman of the Douglass Leadership Institute and the Frederick Douglass Foundation National. --- Bishop Dean Nelson is an executive leader and national speaker who currently serves as the Executive Director for Human Coalition Action, the advocacy arm of Human Coalition, a national pro-life non-profit organization serving women and families in metropolitan areas. He is also the Chairman of the Douglass Leadership Institute, which educates, equips, and empowers minority leaders to bring positive change to their communities. To date, DLI has engaged over 400,000 households and distributed over 2 million pieces of voter education literature in key states and districts. Dean is also the National Chairperson of the Frederick Douglass Foundation. Rev. Nelson is the recipient of the RNC’s 2019 Black Republican Trailblazer Award. In addition to spending several years in campus ministry and church planting, he has extensive experience with grass roots engagement and political campaigns on the local, state, and national level. He was recently appointed by Maryland Governor Larry Hogan to serve on the Congressional Frederick Douglass Bicentennial Commission whose mission was to honor the life and legacy of Frederick Douglass. Rev. Nelson has previously served as the Executive Director for various non-profits and has spoken for and worked with a diverse array of groups including the United Way (VIP training graduate), NAACP, Prison Fellowship, the SCLC, the Family Research Council, the Heritage Foundation, and TeenPact Leadership Schools. He has appeared on and been published in a variety of media outlets, including MSNBC, ABC, and USA Today. He and his wife Julia homeschooled their three children, and he has served on the advisory board of the National Black Home Educators. He is a graduate of the University of Virginia.
