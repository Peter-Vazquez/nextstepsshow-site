Next Steps Show July 21, 2023

Duration: 55:14

Today on the Next Steps Show, we are diving deep into the world of women's health with the esteemed OB/GYN, Dr. Ingrid Skop. Boasting a track record of delivering over 5,000 babies and personally executing more than a dozen lifesaving surgeries for women impacted by the adverse effects of the abortion pill, Dr. Skop's expertise is unparalleled. Her pioneering research is at the forefront of tackling issues related to women's health and the preborn, effectively challenging a politically charged war on women. Dr. Ingrid Skop is the Vice President and Director of Medical Affairs for the prestigious Charlotte Lozier Institute (lozierinstitute.org).
