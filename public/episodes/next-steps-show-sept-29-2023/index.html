Episode Details

This week, our guest is the Hon. Rev. James R. Harden, M.Div., a remarkable individual dedicated to making a difference. As the CEO of CompassCare Pregnancy Services, Rev. Harden has pioneered a transformative medical model that has significantly impacted pregnancy centers nationwide. Living just outside Rochester, NY with his large and loving family, he has also been a vocal advocate for women and pre-born babies, especially following a harrowing incident where CompassCare's Buffalo office faced a brutal attack. His extensive knowledge on medical ethics and pro-life strategy, coupled with his executive leadership, has led him to be featured on numerous national platforms. Stay tuned for an insightful conversation filled with hope, resilience, and the power of community.
