Next Steps Show Nov 3, 2023

Duration: 55:12

This week, we are joined, once again, by a proud US Army Veteran who has shown deep commitment to our community. He serves on the Gates zoning board of appeals and is a former member of its planning board. With a career spanning over three decades in automotive management, he is the esteemed owner of ROC Auto Service and Tire LLC. Central to every endeavor of his is a steadfast faith in God. This is clearly reflected in his active membership at Lakeshore Community Church and his unwavering dedication to volunteering both his time and resources. Now, he's taking another significant step by running as the candidate for Monroe County Legislator District 27. Please join me in welcoming David Ferris. Join us for an enlightening conversation, infused with hope, resilience, and the transformative power of community. #NextStepsShow #PuttingPeopleFirst #WYSL NextStepsShow.com wysl1040.com
