Peter Vazquez Unfiltered: God, Country, and the Fight They Don’t Want You to Win

Duration: 57:51

Peter Vazquez, host of The Next Steps Show, joins Just An Old Guy With A Mic for a no-holds-barred conversation that punches through the noise and gets to the rot beneath the surface of American life. In a world where public officials hide behind buzzwords and bureaucrats choke progress with paperwork, Vazquez speaks plainly about what’s broken and who’s benefiting from the decay. From the failures of housing policy and tax tyranny, to the cowardice of so-called leaders and the cultural con that’s dividing America by race, Peter lays bare the truth that polite society avoids. He doesn’t speak from theory, he speaks from lived service, both on the battlefield and in the trenches of local leadership. This isn’t some polished soundbite. It’s a wake-up call. If you’ve felt the weight of a system rigged against working families, if you’ve seen the cost of silence, or if you’re just tired of being told to sit down and take it this episode is for you. Stream it now. Share it boldly. And remember: A leader leads. A follower follows. Everyone else gets out of the way.
