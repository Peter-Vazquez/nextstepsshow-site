Episode Details

This week, we extend a warm welcome to someone truly extraordinary. She's not only triumphed over breast cancer but now passionately advocates for the cause as a Mary Kay Breast Cancer Ambassador. Through her "Pink October Podcast," she illuminates the journeys of many, weaving together tales of challenges and victories. Every year, she dedicates herself to raising funds in support of both The American Cancer Society and The Mary Kay Breast Cancer Foundation. Join us in welcoming our esteemed guest, Ambassador Patricia Anderson, as we delve into her journey from diagnosis to survivor, and from ambassador to host of the Pink October Podcast.
