In today’s episode of The Next Steps Show, we take a fresh perspective with guest hosts Meg Allocco-Angell and Lauren Demo. As moms, advocates, and homeschool leaders, Meg and Lauren bring heartfelt insight and relatable experiences to the table. Together, they dive into the joys and challenges of motherhood, their work as leaders in the homeschool community, and their commitment to conservative values. From navigating politics to shaping the next generation with intention and faith, this conversation offers both inspiration and practical wisdom for listeners seeking to make a difference in their families and communities. Do not miss this engaging and thought-provoking discussion led by two dynamic women who are living out their values every day. #PuttingPeopleFirst
