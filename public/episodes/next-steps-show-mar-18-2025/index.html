Faith-Driven Entrepreneurship: Reclaiming Business, Family, and Freedom In an era where government overreach and cultural shifts threaten the foundations of faith, family, and financial independence, a new wave of entrepreneurs is rising. These bold leaders refuse to conform to broken systems, choosing instead to build businesses by God’s design, not by the world’s standards. On today’s episode of The Next Steps Show, host Peter Vazquez welcomes faith-driven entrepreneur and movement maker Kristin Dronchi for a no-holds-barred conversation about the fight for economic and cultural freedom. As institutions weaponize corporate power, government regulations, and Big Tech censorship to silence voices of faith and tradition, Kingdom entrepreneurs are forging an alternative economy—one rooted in biblical values, family restoration, and financial sovereignty. The battle isn’t just economic—it’s political and spiritual. Traditional family values are under attack as policies devalue motherhood and promote government dependency. Meanwhile, businesses are pressured to comply with radical corporate agendas that contradict faith and freedom. Dronchi challenges the narrative that success is found in chasing worldly approval, instead advocating for faith-based business models that empower families and communities. With cancel culture, de-banking, and ESG-driven compliance on the rise, how do faith-driven business owners fight back? Through Kingdom collaboration, community-driven entrepreneurship, and the creation of a parallel economy that isn’t shackled to government-controlled systems. Today’s discussion exposes the biggest political and economic lies told to entrepreneurs and provides a roadmap for breaking free from this control. This isn’t just about business—it’s about building a movement. A movement that restores families, reshapes culture, and reclaims economic power from institutions that seek to silence and control. The Next Steps Show is more than a conversation—it’s a call to action. Tune in at noon and be part of the movement.
