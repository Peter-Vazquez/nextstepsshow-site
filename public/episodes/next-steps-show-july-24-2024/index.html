Next Steps Show July 24, 2024

Duration: 49:21

In this episode of The Next Steps Show, we welcome a dynamic guest who has spent years blending faith, creativity, and humor to engage millions across the digital landscape. Señor AlfonZo Rachel—known simply as Zo—joins us to share his journey as a musician, filmmaker, movie star, and Christian conservative commentator. From the early days of MySpace to his widely viewed content on YouTube, Zo’s unique voice continues to inspire and challenge audiences. We dive into his personal story, uncovering the man behind the creativity, and discuss the challenges he has faced, including being shadowbanned on YouTube and suspended on Twitter. Together, we tackle topics like the current political landscape and its implications for faith-driven creators, while Zo offers his perspective on the roles of Biden, Kamala, and Trump in shaping the nation’s future. This episode is a powerful reminder of the resilience needed to speak boldly and remain true to your values in an ever-changing world. Do not miss this conversation with Señor AlfonZo Rachel on finding purpose, navigating challenges, and taking the next steps forward. #PuttingPeopleFirst
