Episode Details

This week on the Next Steps Show, we are pleased to announce our joining forces with Mike Hennessy, host of Good News Talk, for a captivating two-hour Easter special filled with talks about faith, politics and entrepreneurship. Be sure not to miss it! Mike Hennessy has an impressive background, having grown up in Geneva, NY, where he graduated from DeSales High in 1976. He went on to join the NY Army National Guard, and in 1979, he was selected to attend Officer Candidate School, where he was commissioned as a 2nd Lt at the age of 19. Mr. Hennessy then worked in sales and marketing for 15 years before becoming the Director of Marketing for Wright-Wisner Distributing in Rochester in 1993. In 1997, Mike ventured into the world of radio and media marketing at Entercom Rochester, followed by ClearChannel and WHAM 1180 in 2005. In 2009, he accepted the position of Open-Door Mission's first Development Director, and two years later, he was promoted to Executive Director, where he grew the organization's resources significantly, while also establishing the first women and children's housing program in the 65-year-old ministry. In 2020, Mike assumed the role of Executive Director at Youth for Christ Rochester, a faith-led youth development organization in downtown Rochester. Mike and his wife, Cathy, attend Bethel Christian Fellowship, and their son, Joel, serves in the US Air Force.
