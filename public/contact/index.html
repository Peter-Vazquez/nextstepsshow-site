```
Before You Call or Text

A clear message helps the show respond more effectively. If you are suggesting a topic, include the issue, why it matters now, and what kind of conversation you believe would help listeners.

If you are reaching out about guest booking or sponsorship, include your name, organization, contact information, relevant links, and the main reason for your inquiry.

Helpful Details

  • Your name
  • Best phone number
  • Reason for contacting the show
  • Topic or question
  • Relevant website or media links
  • Best time to follow up
```
